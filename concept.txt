Doctor reviews + signs

Workflow C: LIMS sample journey
Phlebotomy list → scan patient wristband + tube barcode (image/QR verification)
Collection recorded → specimen tracked

Pathologist validates → dispatch with audit
Workflow D: TPA pre-auth / claim
OCR insurer card + TPA form
AI compiles required documents from chart (notes, investigations, estimates)
AI drafts narrative + checklist → staff uploads + submits
Denial reason → AI suggests correction packet

To achieve ERP-grade sophistication, your system must logically separate:

Layer A — Revenue Intent (Clinical Billing Layer)

What was planned to be charged?

What was actually consumed?

Variable vs fixed allocation
Layer D — Settlement & Distribution Layer

Who gets what and when?

Doctor share calculation

Advance settlement
